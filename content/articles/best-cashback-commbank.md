## What is Cashback Redemption?

Cashback redemption means using your credit card points to get **money back** or reduce your credit card balance.  
For example, if you earn **2 points for every $1 spent** and **20,000 points = $100 cashback**:

**Spend $10,000 → earn 20,000 points → get $100 cashback.**

Knowing roughly how much you spend each month makes it easier to compare cards, because each card has different **earning rates, annual fees and cashback values**.

Made on 16 August 2026

### What is happening ???

Starting 1 October 2026, The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) will change card rules.  
**Credit cards**: The bank fee drops from 0.80% to **0.30%**  
**Debit cards** : The bank fee drops from 0.20% to **0.16%**

### For buyer/ Card holder

No more card surcharge !

### For Shop/retails and Business

You cannot charge customers more for using cards.  
Banks must lower the fees they charge you.

### What is NOT changing ?

**Business cards**: Work and Company cards still have fees.  
**Other Apps**: PayPal and Afterpay can still charge extra.

Here is my ranking of the major Australian credit card providers based on how much their rewards, bonuses, fees or benefits are changing.

**Summary of the credit card changes happening before 1 October 2026.**

### Major Cuts & Devaluations

**MyCard**: Points lose **62% of their value**. A $100 gift card increases from 20,900 to **33,770 points**. The Prestige card is **discontinued**.  
**ANZ**: Sign-up bonuses slashed. Frequent Flyer Black drops from 130,000 to **80,000 points**. Platinum drops from 75,000 to **40,000 points**. All cashback offers ($100–$200) are **removed**.  
**Virgin Money**: Double penalty. Velocity Flyer points drop from 0.66 to **0.55 per $1**, while the fee rises to **$169**. Velocity High Flyer points drop from 1.0 to **0.75 per $1**, while the fee rises to **$349**.  
**BOQ** : Platinum Visa points are **cut in half** from 2 points to **1 point per $1**. Interest rate increases to **21.99%**.  
**NAB**: Owns and manages the networks for MyCard, Virgin Money, and BOQ. Expect broad changes across its ecosystem.  
**Westpac**: Lower monthly caps introduced. You will earn fewer points if you spend a lot.

### Banks to Watch Closely

**CommBank, St.George, Bank of Melbourne, BankSA**: No major changes confirmed yet, but under heavy pressure.  
**Bendigo, Macquarie, HSBC**: No confirmed cuts yet. Safe for now, but keep monitoring.

### The Strongest Option

**American Express (Amex)**: Not affected by the new bank fee rules. The **Amex Explorer** still offers an uncapped **2 points per $1**.  
BUT  
Additional/employee cardholders will **lose Priority Pass access** starting October 2026 .Additionally, the Amex Qantas Ultimate card is losing its access passes to domestic **Centurion Lounges**.

_Note: These rankings are based on confirmed changes announced for 1 October 2026. Other banks may still announce cuts as the deadline approaches._

Complimentary travel insurance can be valuable, but cover is not automatic in every situation. The policy wording—not the card's marketing page—determines whether a claim is eligible.

## Confirm how cover is activated

Some policies require you to pay for return travel using the card. Others require a minimum prepaid travel cost or allow points bookings only when taxes are charged to the card.

Check the activation rule before paying a deposit, especially when travellers in the same booking use different cards.

## Check who is covered

Partners and dependent children may be included only when they travel with the cardholder or meet specific residency and age conditions. Additional cardholders can also have different eligibility rules.

## Read exclusions and limits

Review pre-existing medical condition rules, maximum trip duration, age limits, excluded activities, rental vehicle cover, cancellation limits, and the excess payable for each claim.

Cover for valuables can include per-item sub-limits that are much lower than the total baggage limit. Smartphones, cameras, and jewellery deserve particular attention.

## Keep evidence

Save the policy document that applied when you booked, along with receipts, itineraries, card statements, medical reports, and reports for theft or loss. Insurers may require prompt notification and supporting documents.

If the included policy does not fit your trip, compare standalone cover before departure rather than assuming the card policy will fill every gap.
